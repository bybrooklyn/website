---
name: Climb
summary: A block, a hammer, and a long way up.
kind: game
space: 2D
controls: Click to take the hammer. Its head follows the mouse. Press it against something and you are the one who moves. Escape lets go of the mouse.
order: 3
---

There is nothing to press but the mouse. Hook the hammer over a ledge and pull yourself up to it, or plant it under you and push off. There are no places that save how far you have come: slip, and you are wherever you land.

Clicking holds the mouse inside the box and makes the box bigger, so the hammer cannot wander off the edge of it. Escape gives the mouse back.

It is a small homage to a much better game about a hammer and a mountain. The arms are one motor in Mote's physics that pushes the hammer's head toward the mouse and pushes the block back just as hard.
