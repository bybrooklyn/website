## What it is

YouTube and YouTube Music for the Mac, in the style of the YouTube app on a TV. It runs full screen and you move around with a remote, controller, keyboard or trackpad. You can add it to Steam and launch it like a game.

It is a native SwiftUI app. It is not made by or connected to YouTube or Google.

## What's in it

- Home, Subscriptions, Library, channels, playlists and search
- A video player up to 1080p, with captions, speed, audio tracks, Up Next, chapters, transcripts and comments
- Shorts
- YouTube Music, with a queue and synced lyrics
- SponsorBlock and DeArrow
- Sign-in with a code you approve in your browser, so you never type your password into the app

## Status

Some of this is rough, and YouTube can break things whenever it changes something. It needs macOS 26 or newer. The repository is not public yet.
