## What it is

A native YouTube Music client written in Rust, Qt 6 (through cxx-qt) and Kirigami. No browser engine, no Electron, no FFmpeg, no system OpenSSL.

It looks and behaves like a normal KDE application: standard Kirigami and Breeze controls, nothing hand-drawn.

## What it does

- **Browse and play.** Home and Explore as shelves of cards; album, playlist and artist pages; library, search, queue, shuffle, repeat and radio.
- **Lyrics.** Three providers are asked in parallel and line-synced lyrics always win. The current line is tinted with the cover's color and filled as it is sung.
- **Downloads.** Per song, album or playlist, plus opt-in smart downloads within a storage limit. Downloaded songs play from disk.
- **Mini-player and system tray**, MPRIS media keys, and configurable keyboard shortcuts.

## The audio engine

Qt Multimedia plays audio by default. You can switch to the built-in engine, which is pure Rust, for gapless playback, crossfade, loudness normalization and a 10-band equalizer with presets.

## Building it

You need `just`, Rust, Qt 6 and Kirigami. Then `just run` builds and runs it, and `just install` puts it in `~/.local/bin` with a desktop entry.
