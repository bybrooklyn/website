## What it is

A page of nine sliders: gender identity, gender expression, sexual and romantic orientation, sexual attraction, sexual drive, romantic desire, relationship attitude and gender comfort. Each has seventeen positions and can be left unset. A slider moves smoothly while it is dragged, and settles on the nearest position when it is let go. It is in English, Polish, German, Portuguese and Arabic.

The share link carries every slider's value in an eight-character code, and the language when it is not English. Opening the link shows those values.

## Nothing is stored

There are no accounts, no cookies and no tracking. The values are kept in the page's address and nowhere else.

## Where it comes from

It is made after Spectrum by [Andrea Vos](https://avris.it), and is used under the [Opinionated Queer License v1.1](https://oql.avris.it/license/v1.1). The idea, the axes, their wording and the translations come from the original, and the translators are credited above the sliders when their language is picked.

## What was changed

- **Rewritten for this site**, in its own code and design, with no libraries.
- **Two of the original's eleven sliders taken out**, in every language.
- **Seventeen positions** on each slider where the original has nine: its nine, and one halfway between each pair. Only the words at the ends and the middle are shown.
- **New share codes**, eight characters for the nine sliders. Links with the original's seven-character codes still open, at the same places and without the two sliders taken out.
