## What it is

A page of nine sliders: gender identity, gender expression, sexual and romantic orientation, sexual attraction, sexual drive, romantic desire, relationship attitude and gender comfort. Each goes from 1 to 9 and can be left unset. A chart beside them averages the sliders into five corners and changes shape as they move. It is in English, Polish, German, Portuguese and Arabic.

The share link carries every slider's value in a six-character code, and the language when it is not English. Opening the link shows those values.

## Nothing is stored

There are no accounts, no cookies and no tracking. The values are kept in the page's address and nowhere else.

## Where it comes from

It is made after Spectrum by [Andrea Vos](https://avris.it), and is used under the [Opinionated Queer License v1.1](https://oql.avris.it/license/v1.1). The idea, the axes, their wording and the translations come from the original, and the translators are credited on the page.
