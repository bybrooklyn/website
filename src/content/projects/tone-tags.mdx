A tone tag goes at the end of a message, after a slash, and says how to read it. "Nice hat /j" is a joke. "Nice hat /gen" means it.

Writing drops the voice and the face that carry tone. Some people, many of them neurodivergent, find tone in text hard to read at all, and a tag takes the guessing out of it.

Nobody has to memorise these. Ask what one means when you don't know it, and say so when you miss one.
