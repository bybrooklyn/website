## The short version

| What | What you may do |
|---|---|
| My writing, photographs, pictures and resume | Share them and make things from them, if you credit me and it is not to make money. |
| Photographs marked "by Dylan" | The same, but they are his. Credit him. |
| The shitposts on the photos page | They are not mine, so I can't give you permission for them. |
| This site's code | Nearly anything, including selling what you make. Keep my copyright notice, and don't pass yourself or your copy off as me. |
| Mote, the engine the minis run on | Anything. Keep the copyright notice. |
| The fonts | Use them and change them. They can't be sold by themselves. |

## Credit

Credit means my name and a link to where you found it. For example:

> Photograph by Charlotte Halmstad, [bybrooklyn.dev/photos](https://bybrooklyn.dev/photos/), CC BY-NC 4.0

For one of Dylan's, write his name in place of mine.

## Not to make money

A school project, a blog post, a wallpaper, a zine you give away: all fine. An advert, a product, a stock photo site, a book you sell: ask me first at [you@bybrooklyn.dev](mailto:you@bybrooklyn.dev). I usually say yes.

## Don't pretend to be me

You can copy this whole site's code and make your own from it. You can't say your copy is mine, use my name to promote it, or put words in my mouth that I did not write.
