It starts knowing nothing. Each generation is thirty-two brains, and each one is given the same body on the same ground for ten seconds. It is scored by how far its body got, less a little for every second it lay on the ground and for the effort its muscles spent. The best four go on to the next generation unchanged, and the rest are their children, mixed and changed a little at random.

All of it runs in your browser. The learning happens in the background, and the page shows the best walker so far, with the whole generation faintly behind it, most of them falling over. In the corner is its brain: senses on the left, joints on the right, the lines lit by what passes along them as it walks.

The body and its physics are written in Rust and run as WebAssembly. Change the legs or the ground and it starts learning again from nothing. "Copy link to this walker" puts its brain in the link, so whoever opens it gets the same walker, already trained.
