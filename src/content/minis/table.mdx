The dice are real solids. They are dropped in at the top with a spin, hit the ramps and each other, tumble and roll out into the tray, and the number is whichever face ends up on top. Nothing is decided beforehand. The tests roll each kind of die hundreds of times and check that every face comes up as often as chance says it should. A die that stops inside the tower is given a tap, as you would tap a real one.

Under the tower there are two views. **Fight** is for whoever runs the game: names, initiative, whose turn it is, hit points and conditions. **Character** is for one player: hit points, death saves, conditions and a few rolls kept ready. All of it stays in this browser.

The conditions are the fifteen from the fifth edition. A d100 is two ten-sided dice, tens and ones.
