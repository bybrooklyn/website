Gold stars pull the comet, blue pushers push it, and a wormhole sends it out of its twin at the same speed and heading. Leaving the field, hitting anything or flying for twelve seconds is a miss, and every launch counts as a shot. Each field is made from a seed and its number, so a link opens the same field for anyone, and it is only kept once the game has found a launch that wins it.
