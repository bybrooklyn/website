A plain page for watching Mote's marbles roll, made for the marble run that is planned. The track is worked out by formula rather than built of faces, so a marble feels no seams, and the marbles roll as real ones do, slower on two rails than in a gutter and slower again than sliding. It is drawn plainly; the look of wood and glass comes later.
