Each grain is one cell of a grid, and each material follows a few rules of its own. Sand piles up, water levels out, and steam rises and rains back down. Fire climbs wood and leaves ash. Lava hardens into stone as it cools and boils the water it meets. Acid eats through stone and is used up.

Pride sand is sand in the colors of a flag. Drawn in a line, it lays the flag's stripes side by side, and "whole flag" lays a flag in one piece. Every grain keeps its stripe as it falls, so the stripes are still there in the heap.

"Copy link to this world" puts the whole world in the link, and whoever opens it gets it as it was, paused. A world too big for a link is saved as a file instead, which opens again when it is dropped on the mini.

The world is written in Rust and runs as WebAssembly. The same world poured the same way comes out the same on every machine.
