Each grain is one cell of a grid. Sand piles up, dirt holds a steeper slope, water levels out and finds the same height in joined vessels, steam rises and gathers under whatever is above it, and stone stays where it is put. Heavier things sink through lighter ones.

The world is written in Rust and runs as WebAssembly. The same world poured the same way comes out the same on every machine.
