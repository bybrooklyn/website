name: Plinko
summary: A table of posts that makes passwords.
kind: toy
space: 3D
controls: Press drop, or click the table. Each ball is a character, and the order they reach the bin in is the password.
panel: true
order: 7
---

Every ball is a character. They are let go one after another at the top of a sloped table, roll and knock their way down between the posts, and drop off the end into a bin. The password is the balls in the order they got there. When the last one has settled, each character lifts off its ball and they line up in that order.

Each ball's character comes from the browser's secure random source when the ball is made, so every character is as likely as any other. The table only decides the order, and a fair character is still fair whatever order it arrives in. The balls are real spheres on Mote's 3D physics: they roll, hit the posts and each other, and pile up in the bin.

The length, the kinds of character and the table are yours to set. The table settings change how it looks and sounds, not how strong the password is. Your settings are kept in this browser. The password is not kept anywhere.
