summary: A Plinko board that makes passwords.

controls: Press drop, or click the board. Each ball that lands sends the character in its bin up to the password.
panel: true
order: 7
---

Every bin shows a character. The balls drop one after another, and the pegs light up as they are hit. When a ball lands in a bin, that bin's character flies up to the next empty place in the password along the top, and the bin gets a new one.

A Plinko board is not fair. A ball ends up in some bins far more often than others. So the characters in the bins come from the browser's secure random source, and where a ball lands has nothing to do with what the bin holds. Every character is as likely as any other. The tests drop 6,400 balls on four different boards and check that.

The length, the kinds of character and the board are yours to set. The board settings change how it looks and sounds, not how strong the password is. Your settings are kept in this browser. The password is not kept anywhere.
