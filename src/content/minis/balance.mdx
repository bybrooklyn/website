---
name: Balance
summary: Roll the ball onto each mark before time runs out, and do not let it fall.
kind: game
space: 2D
controls: Move the mouse left and right to tilt the beam, or use the arrow keys or A and D. Click or press Space to start.
order: 2
---

Each mark you reach buys time. The clock runs faster with every one, the gusts get stronger and the beam gets shorter. It ends when the ball falls or the time is gone. Your best score is kept in this browser.
