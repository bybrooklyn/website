### Why I love it

This is one of the best games I've ever played, and it changed my perspective on consciousness. A machine could be a person. If it acts, wants and suffers like one, there is nothing left to tell it apart by. It lands with me personally because of my own introspective thoughts on being alive.

What stays with me is Markus, and Connor and Hank.

### My history with it

My first time through, Markus stayed peaceful and it worked, and Connor became a deviant and was friends with Hank at the end. I'm in the middle of machine Connor right now and it hurts!
