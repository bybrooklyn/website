## Why I love it

I play it with friends. I love its humor and its writing, and the soundtrack, which is by [RENREN](/interests/renren/). Modding it is half the fun.

## My history with it

A random friend on Steam bought it for me. It sat dormant until September, when I got fucking hooked. Funny enough, before that I had found the soundtrack randomly on YT Music.

I wanted multiplayer, so I started modding it. The mod is [Novean](/projects/novean/).
