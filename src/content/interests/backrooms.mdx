### Why I love it

I LOVE the Backrooms and its web series SO MUCH. It's perfect and I love every bit and piece about it. The lore is addicting.

The idea itself: endless yellow rooms, the hum of the lights, a place you fall into. And this is it made into a feature, by the person who made [the web series](/interests/backrooms-series/). His music has [a screen too](/interests/kane-parsons/).
