Metal on a 486: guitars and chiptune. The titles are half the fun: C:\>Fixmbr, Virus.DOS, Interrupt Request.

### My history with it

I found him through Ultrakill. The same man is [KEYGEN CHURCH](/interests/keygen-church/), who wrote music for it, and I followed the name. I listen to it all the time.
