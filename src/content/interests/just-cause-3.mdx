## Why I love it

Getting around is the game. You grapple, parachute and wingsuit everywhere, and you tether things together. I love blowing everything up, and I love the islands. And the story! Rico coming home to free his own country, taking down Di Ravello, Mario and Dimah and the rest. It never takes itself seriously.

## My history with it

I first played it around when it came out. I have over 500 hours in it and I've 100%ed the game twice. Now I mostly free roam and wreck things, or wingsuit and do the challenges. I replay the story too.
