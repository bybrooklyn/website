Building and automating. Exploring new planets and caves, and digging to the core. Playing it co-op. I like the look of it, and the calm: nothing rushes you.

## My history with it

I've reached the core of every planet, and I've beaten the game 5 times. I like to start over often after beating it. I play it with one friend.
