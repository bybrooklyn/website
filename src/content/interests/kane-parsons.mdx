### Why I love it

All music from Kane Parsons is very excellent. I listen to it on its own, as music: the Backrooms scores, the film's soundtrack, his own albums, The Oldest View.

It is [the Backrooms](/interests/backrooms-series/). The series would be half of what it is without his score.
