## Why I love it

It's perfect and I love every bit and piece about it. The lore is addicting.

Kane Pixels' series is found footage, Async, a story told through tapes and documents. Around it there is the whole world of the Backrooms: the levels, the wikis, the games, everything people built.

## My history with it

It started for me with Kane Pixels' first video, The Backrooms (Found Footage), and with the Wikidot wiki, until I became a diehard fan of only Kane Parsons' version.

[The film](/interests/backrooms/) and [his music](/interests/kane-parsons/) have pages of their own.
