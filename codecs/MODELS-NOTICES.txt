

================ Models imgQuire offers to repair and enlarge pictures ================

These are not in this folder and are never served by this site. When they are chosen, the visitor's browser
downloads each from Hugging Face at the commit named here and keeps it in its own storage.

Better, for repairing a JPEG: SCUNet (real-world, PSNR) by Kai Zhang, Yawei Li, Jingyun Liang, Jiezhang Cao,
Yulun Zhang, Hao Tang, Deng-Ping Fan, Radu Timofte and Luc Van Gool, from https://github.com/cszn/SCUNet,
licensed under the Apache License 2.0 there. Converted to ONNX at half precision by colpona:
https://huggingface.co/colpona/dejpeg-models, commit 3cc3250ce83e61bffdc1c6ffcfb27161951f5e9f,
scunet/scunet_color_real_psnr_fp16.onnx (that repository is tagged Apache-2.0 as a whole).

Best, for repairing a JPEG: FBCNN (colour) by Jiaxi Jiang, Kai Zhang and Radu Timofte, from
https://github.com/jiaxi-jiang/FBCNN, licensed under the Apache License 2.0 there. Converted to ONNX at half
precision by colpona: https://huggingface.co/colpona/dejpeg-models, commit
3cc3250ce83e61bffdc1c6ffcfb27161951f5e9f, fbcnn/fbcnn_color_fp16.onnx.

Sharp, for enlarging: Swin2SR (lightweight, twice as large) by Marcos V. Conde, Ui-Jin Choi, Maxime Burchi and
Radu Timofte, from https://github.com/mv-lab/swin2sr, licensed under the Apache License 2.0 there. Converted to
ONNX by Xenova: https://huggingface.co/Xenova/swin2SR-lightweight-x2-64, commit
92a21aca5713f20faf9a87590cdfbdce2e34112c, onnx/model_fp16.onnx (tagged Apache-2.0).

The Apache License 2.0 is at https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.
