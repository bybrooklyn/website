its own and one of imgQuire's tools (`imgQuire.md`).

Nothing yet. Nothing is built.

## To ask her

- **What "no bullshit" rules out:** an account, a limit on how many, a watermark, a smaller picture unless
  paid for, an upload to someone's server, a wait. So: drop or paste a picture, the cut-out appears, copy it
  or save it as a PNG at its full size. Nothing leaves the browser, which is also what makes it free to run:
  the site stays static and there is no server to pay for.
- **How it would work:** a segmentation model fetched into the browser once and run there, as mdQuire's
  dictation does (ONNX Runtime is already the one library). This model picks out what is already in the
  picture and invents nothing, which is a different thing from making images; to ask her that this sits on
  the right side of "i do not condone the usage of ai images".
- **To ask:** which model and its licence (some of the good ones are free only for non-commercial use: is
  her site that); how big a download is acceptable (they run from about 5 MB to 170 MB, better edges costing
  more); hair and fur edges; many pictures at once; a brush to fix what it got wrong (keep, remove); a new
  background (clear, a colour, a blur of the old one, another picture); the picture's full size or a limit;
  phones.
- **Where it lives:** a page of its own that does the one thing, and the same piece inside imgQuire (27) as
  one of its tools. Build the piece once.
